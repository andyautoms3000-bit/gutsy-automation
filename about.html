Our story

Born from engineering.
Built for business.

Gutsy Automation began with a simple belief: technology should solve real problems.

Founded in Nairobi in 2026, Gutsy brings together software, automation and engineering thinking to help businesses improve the way they work.

Our approach is shaped by an engineering background where automation means more than software. It means understanding a system, identifying what can be improved, and designing a practical way to make it work better.

Today, that thinking is being applied to software, business automation and digital systems, with a longer-term vision that extends into industrial automation.

Our vision

Technology without borders.

To help businesses around the world use technology to operate better, solve meaningful problems and pursue new possibilities.

Our mission

To turn real-world business problems into practical technology that creates room for businesses to grow.

We combine software, automation and engineering thinking to simplify complex processes, connect systems and build tools that work in the real world.

Our principle

Founder

Sir Andrew Omwache (Gutsy)

Founder

An innovative technologist with a strong interest in technology and problem solving, Sir Andrew Omwache founded Gutsy Automation with a belief that technology can be applied practically to solve real-world problems.

An award-winning mechatronics technician with a record of achievement at regional and national level, his technical foundation began in mechatronics and industrial automation.

That engineering foundation eventually extended into software, automation and digital systems — forming the basis of Gutsy's approach today.

For Andrew, solving problems through technology is not simply an area of interest; it is a way of thinking.

Technology is not just about what we build, but the problems we help solve.
— Sir Andrew OmwacheFounder, Gutsy Automation
