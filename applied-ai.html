HOW ENGAGEMENTS WORK

Assess first. Engineer to the actual requirement.

Every Applied AI engagement begins with an assessment. We establish the business objective, technical environment, data requirements, integrations, security constraints, expected usage and operational responsibilities before confirming scope, price and delivery timeline.

01 · ASSESS

Understand the requirement

Review workflows, data, systems, infrastructure, users, security and expected outcomes.

02 · DEFINE

Set the scope

Specify capabilities, integrations, hosting configuration, permissions, usage and acceptance criteria.

03 · ENGINEER

Build and integrate

Configure models, knowledge, agents, channels, workflows and managed infrastructure as agreed.

04 · VALIDATE

Test behaviour and controls

Test expected tasks, response quality, failure scenarios, access rules and escalation paths.

05 · DEPLOY

Launch and hand over

Deploy the agreed system, document operations and onboard the relevant users.

06 · MANAGE

Monitor and maintain

Provide the agreed recurring infrastructure, monitoring, maintenance and support.

Managed hosting: Gutsy manages the hosting and infrastructure arrangement by default. The technical design and hosting footprint are established during assessment. External provider and consumption charges remain separately itemised.

COMMERCIAL CONDITIONS

Clear separation between engineering, service and consumption.

Setup

One-time engineering, configuration, integration, testing and deployment charge for the agreed scope.

Monthly subscription

Recurring managed services, infrastructure, monitoring, maintenance and support within the agreed subscription scope.

Third-party consumption

AI model/API usage, WhatsApp, SMS, telephony, cloud resources and other external provider costs are itemised separately where applicable.

Additional scope

Custom development and integrations outside the agreed scope may require a separate quotation.
