The opportunity

Understand how campaign rewards work.

Clipping campaigns are offers from brands or creators. A campaign defines the source content, accepted platforms, posting requirements and reward model. Your results depend on campaign terms and the performance of eligible posts.

CPM

Paid per 1,000 views

For a CPM campaign, the reward is calculated using eligible views and the campaign’s stated rate.

Per Post

Fixed post reward

Some campaigns pay a stated amount for an eligible post rather than paying by view count.

Retainer

Agreed campaign work

Some campaigns use an agreed recurring or package-based arrangement. Always read the campaign terms.

Illustrative CPM calculation: 10,000 eligible views at a $2 CPM = $20 gross campaign earnings before any applicable platform fee, campaign cap or other terms. This is an example, not a promise or forecast of income.

02

Prepare your Groq account and API key

Connect your Groq API key to enable the AI processing used by Guava Clip. Create your own key and keep it private.

  1. Open console.groq.com and create an account.
  2. Open the API Keys page.
  3. Choose Create API Key, give it a recognisable name such as “Guava Clip”, then create it.
  4. Copy the key and store it securely. Do not send it to anyone or include it in screenshots.
  5. If you lose the key, create a replacement and update the tool’s settings.
03

Install and configure the tool

Use the installation file and instructions delivered after your purchase. Follow the prompts shown by the installer.

  1. Download the delivered tool file onto your computer.
  2. Open the installer and follow its on-screen installation steps.
  3. Launch Guava Clip.
  4. Open Settings, paste your Groq API key into the designated field, and save.
  5. Run the tool’s test clip if that option is available in your delivered version. Confirm it completes before moving on.

If the installer or settings screen differs from the screenshot, stop and contact one-on-one support rather than guessing at settings.

04

Create a dedicated TikTok account

Use an account that you intend to connect to Content Rewards. Campaign submission rules may require the account to be public.

  1. Create or sign in to your TikTok account.
  2. Complete the profile with a recognisable profile image and a suitable bio.
  3. Set the account to Public in TikTok privacy settings if required by the campaign platform.
05

Create your Content Rewards account

Open contentrewards.com and complete its registration flow. The platform may route account creation through Whop.

  1. Open Content Rewards.
  2. Choose the sign-up option and complete the requested account details.
  3. Finish any account verification prompts shown to you.
06

Connect your TikTok account

Use the current connected-account flow inside Content Rewards. The platform interface may change, so follow the labels currently shown in your account.

  1. Open your Content Rewards profile or account settings.
  2. Choose Connected accounts.
  3. Choose Connect account, select TikTok, then sign in and approve the requested connection.
  4. Return to Content Rewards and confirm that TikTok appears as connected or verified.

07

Find and join a suitable campaign

Campaigns can have different reward structures and conditions. Read the campaign page before using its source material.

  1. Open Discover in Content Rewards.
  2. Open a campaign and check its reward model: CPM, Per Post or Retainer.
  3. Read the accepted platforms, content rules, disclosure requirements, minimum payout, budget or cap, and submission deadline.
  4. Check whether the campaign offers Join Campaign for immediate access or Apply for a review before access.
  5. Only use the campaign’s authorised source material and follow its terms.
08

Generate clips using Guava Clip

Use the source video and campaign brief to configure Guava Clip. The exact controls depend on the version supplied to you.

  1. Open the campaign’s authorised source video or download the supplied source file if the campaign permits it.
  2. In Guava Clip, paste the source link or load the permitted video file.
  3. Read the campaign brief carefully and enter its instructions in the tool’s brief or prompt field where available.
  4. Set the output format and duration to match the campaign requirements. For vertical TikTok content, use 9:16 only where the campaign accepts that format.
  5. Set captions and other available output options according to the campaign brief.
  6. Choose an output folder you can find easily, then start generation.
  7. Wait for the process to finish. Open the output folder and review every generated clip.
  8. Check that each clip follows the campaign brief, uses permitted material, has readable captions, and starts and ends cleanly.
09

Publish the eligible clip

Publish only after checking the campaign’s platform, disclosure, content and approval requirements.

  1. If the campaign requires pre-approval, submit the draft through the campaign process and wait for approval before publishing.
  2. For commercial content, use TikTok’s applicable content disclosure setting when required. Follow the campaign’s exact disclosure instructions.
  3. Publish from the connected account on an accepted platform, using the visibility and post settings required by the campaign.
  4. Copy the public post link after publishing.
10

Submit the post to the campaign

Return to the matching campaign in Content Rewards and submit the public post URL. The platform’s current guidance specifies submission within 30 minutes of posting.

  1. Open the campaign and choose Submit clip.
  2. Paste the public post link.
  3. Confirm that you have read and followed the campaign requirements.
  4. Submit the clip and check that it appears in your submissions.
11

Track review, views and earnings

Open the submissions or earnings area in Content Rewards. A submitted post may remain pending while the platform reviews it.

  1. Check whether the submission is pending, approved or rejected.
  2. If rejected, read the reason and compare it with the campaign requirements before submitting future clips.
  3. For CPM campaigns, distinguish eligible views and calculated earnings from money that is approved and available to withdraw.
  4. Check the campaign’s current earning period, minimum payout and any hold period.
12

Withdraw available earnings

Content Rewards provides withdrawal through Whop Balance when your available balance meets its current minimum. The available payout methods depend on country, account and provider.

  1. Open Earnings in Content Rewards.
  2. Choose Withdraw, then select Whop Balance.
  3. Continue to the payout methods available to your Whop account.
  4. Choose and complete a withdrawal using the payout method available in your account.
