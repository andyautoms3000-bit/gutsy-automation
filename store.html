How to Purchase

Buy through WhatsApp.

Purchases are initiated through WhatsApp.

  1. Select the product you want.
  2. Review its description and fixed price.
  3. Select Buy Now.
  4. WhatsApp opens with a prefilled message identifying the selected product.
  5. Send the message to initiate your purchase.
  6. Receive payment instructions and complete the purchase through the WhatsApp sales process.
  7. Receive the purchased digital product through WhatsApp file delivery.
Example purchase message

“Hello Gutsy Automation, I would like to buy ‘12 Cybersecurity Protection Methods.pdf’. Please guide me through the payment and delivery process.”

The product name is automatically inserted into the message for each purchase.
