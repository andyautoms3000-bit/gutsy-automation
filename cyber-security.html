GUTSY AUTOMATION · SECURITY ENGINEERING

Security engineered around your business.

We engineer cybersecurity solutions that protect business systems, infrastructure, applications, devices and information. From identifying vulnerabilities to implementing defensive controls and responding to active incidents, our focus is reducing risk and strengthening the security of your operations.

We approach cybersecurity with an understanding that security is not only a technical requirement. It protects business continuity, financial assets, customer relationships, intellectual property and organisational trust.

Client privacy is a priority. We understand that engaging a cybersecurity firm requires sharing sensitive information about your business, infrastructure and potential vulnerabilities. We treat that responsibility seriously. We aim to collect only the information necessary for an engagement, restrict access to authorised personnel, protect assessment findings and handle client information confidentially.

We do not use client vulnerabilities, security findings, infrastructure details or incident information as marketing material.

Engineering Principle

Security is an engineering principle, not an afterthought.

Secure by Design applies across all our cybersecurity services and relevant engineering work.

We consider security from the beginning of system design rather than adding it after deployment.

Client Privacy & Confidentiality

Your security information stays confidential.

Cybersecurity work requires access to information that businesses cannot afford to expose. Protecting this information is part of the service itself.

Cybersecurity work requires access to information that businesses cannot afford to expose. This may include infrastructure diagrams, system configurations, vulnerability reports, access arrangements, business records and details of active security incidents.

We recognise that protecting this information is part of the service itself.

Our client privacy commitments include:

  1. Confidential handling

Client information, assessment findings and security reports are treated as confidential business information.

  1. Restricted access

Access to client information should be limited to personnel authorised and assigned to the engagement.

  1. Purpose limitation

Information collected during an engagement is used for the agreed security purpose, not unrelated activities.

  1. Secure information handling

We apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect client information during collection, storage, processing and transfer.

  1. No public exposure of client vulnerabilities

We do not publish client security weaknesses, infrastructure details, incident reports or sensitive findings as promotional content.

  1. Controlled reporting

Security findings and technical reports are shared through agreed channels with authorised recipients.

  1. Responsible retention

Information is retained only as required for the engagement, legitimate operational requirements and applicable legal obligations, with agreed disposal arrangements where appropriate.

  1. Confidentiality agreements

Where required, confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements can be established before sensitive engagement information is exchanged.

Our privacy commitments are supported by appropriate access controls, secure handling procedures and contractual arrangements. We do not treat a confidentiality statement as a substitute for actual security controls.
