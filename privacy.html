Gutsy Automation

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Gutsy Automation ("we", "us") builds WhatsApp and business automation tools for small and medium businesses, including Lucy, an AI receptionist that answers customer messages on WhatsApp. We are based in Nairobi, Kenya. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, and what choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and run our services:

  • WhatsApp details: your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and the content of messages you send to our WhatsApp number or to a business that uses our tools.
  • Details you give us: your name, business name, email address, service you are interested in, and booking or enquiry details such as preferred date and time.
  • Website data: basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, collected when you use gutsyautomation.com.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and answer questions about our services.
  • To book appointments and discovery calls.
  • To follow up on an enquiry you made.
  • To improve how our tools respond and to keep them secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

Automated and AI-assisted replies

Messages you send to our WhatsApp number may be answered by an AI assistant. Message text is processed by AI service providers so that the assistant can generate a reply. A person at Gutsy Automation may review conversations to fix problems or follow up on an enquiry. You can ask to speak to a person at any time.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us deliver the service, and only as needed:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): carries your messages. Meta processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • AI, hosting and storage providers: process or store message and booking data on our behalf.
  • Email and productivity tools: used to notify us of new enquiries.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation and enquiry records only as long as needed to serve you and handle follow-up, and then delete or anonymise them. If you ask us to delete your data sooner, we will.

Your choices and rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to us using it. You can stop messaging us at any time.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, send us a message on WhatsApp saying "delete my data", or contact us through gutsyautomation.com/onboarding and tell us the phone number you used. We will confirm once your data has been removed.

Security

We use access controls and reputable providers to protect your information. No online service is completely secure, but we limit who can access your data and what we store.

Children

Our services are for businesses and their customers. They are not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: reach us through gutsyautomation.com/onboarding or on WhatsApp.
