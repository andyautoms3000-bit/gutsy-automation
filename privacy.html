Privacy Policy

Gutsy Automation

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

At Gutsy Automation, we value privacy, confidentiality and the responsible handling of information.

We recognise that personal information, business records, technical documentation and intellectual property can be sensitive. We approach information entrusted to us with discretion and aim to handle it responsibly throughout our interactions and engagements.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, purchase a digital product or engage with our services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website and services, information may include:

Information you provide

  • Name or business name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Enquiry details and messages.
  • Information about your business, project or requested services.
  • Product purchase enquiries and related communications.
  • Information voluntarily provided during a consultation or engagement.

Technical information

  • IP address and general device or browser information.
  • Website usage and diagnostic information.
  • Pages visited and interactions with website features.
  • Information collected through cookies or similar technologies, where implemented.

Please avoid submitting passwords, financial account credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information through general website enquiry forms.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests.
  • Understand business and project requirements.
  • Prepare proposals, quotations and service recommendations.
  • Process digital product enquiries and purchases.
  • Deliver agreed products and services.
  • Communicate about ongoing engagements.
  • Maintain operational, transaction and support records.
  • Improve website functionality, security and user experience.
  • Identify and address suspicious activity or misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal and contractual requirements.

Information is not intended to be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis.

4. Client Confidentiality

We understand that technical engagements can involve commercially sensitive information, internal business records, intellectual property, infrastructure details and security-related findings.

We value the confidentiality of this information and seek to:

  • Limit access to information according to operational requirements.
  • Use information for the purposes connected with the relevant enquiry or engagement.
  • Handle client documentation and technical information discreetly.
  • Avoid publicly exposing client vulnerabilities, security findings, infrastructure details or incident information as marketing material.
  • Respect agreed confidentiality and non-disclosure arrangements.
  • Avoid unnecessary disclosure of client information.

Additional confidentiality requirements may be established through individual engagement agreements.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be made available to relevant service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, communicate with customers, process transactions, deliver services or maintain technical infrastructure.

Examples may include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Communication and messaging platforms.
  • Payment service providers.
  • Website security and technical service providers.
  • Software, storage and automation providers.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or through valid legal processes.

We do not intend to sell personal information as a commercial product.

Specific third-party providers and integrations depend on the services actually used.

6. Data Storage and Security

We value the security and confidentiality of information.

Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards should be applied to information handled through our website and services. Depending on the nature of the information and the systems involved, these may include access restrictions, authentication controls, secure communications, controlled storage and appropriate backup arrangements.

No internet-based system or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore avoid making absolute guarantees about the security of information.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, fulfilling purchases, delivering services, maintaining relevant records and addressing contractual or legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, appropriate deletion, anonymisation or disposal measures may be applied, subject to applicable obligations and legitimate retention requirements.

8. Cookies and Website Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage or improve security.

Where such technologies are implemented, relevant information about their purpose and available choices should be provided to visitors.

You may also be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functions.

9. External Platforms and Links

The website may link to or interact with external platforms, including WhatsApp and third-party payment or communication services.

When you use an external platform, your interaction may be governed by that provider's own privacy policy, terms and security practices.

Gutsy Automation does not control the independent privacy practices of external platforms.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including the right to:

  • Be informed about how their information is used.
  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or misleading information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Object to certain forms of processing.
  • Exercise other rights available under applicable data-protection law.

Requests concerning personal information may be submitted through the designated privacy contact below.

11. International Data Processing

Some website, communication, storage or software providers may process information using infrastructure located outside Kenya.

Where applicable, international transfers and processing should be subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable legal requirements.

12. Children's Information

Our website and services are generally intended for businesses, professionals and adult users.

We do not intentionally seek to collect children's personal information through general business enquiry forms. If such information is received unintentionally, appropriate steps may be taken to address it.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in website functionality, services, information-handling practices or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with its revision date.
