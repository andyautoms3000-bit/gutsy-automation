HOW IT'S OFFERED

Choose your operating structure.

Select a deployment model based on how your business is organised. Then choose the capability package that fits your requirements.

DEPLOYMENT 01

Single Location

One business operating from one physical location, with connected staff, customers, resources and records.

  • Location-specific staff and operations.
  • Connected business records.
  • Owner visibility across the operation.
DEPLOYMENT 02

Multi Location

Manage multiple branches from one system while retaining branch-level control.

  • Branch-specific records and configuration.
  • Central owner access and oversight.
  • Staff permissions scoped to assigned branches.
  • Branch manager access limited to authorised locations.
  • Individual, comparison and consolidated reporting.
  • Shared customer history where configured.
  • Stock and resource transfers where applicable.

Multi Location is a deployment model, not a fourth package. Branch and resource capabilities apply where relevant to the industry.
