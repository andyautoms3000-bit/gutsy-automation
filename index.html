01 · Business management systems

Business OS

Industry-specific business management systems that bring essential functions together for one location or multiple locations.

  • Business Operations: Bookings, orders, customer records, staff management, roles, permissions and automated workflows.
  • Finance and Accounting: Accounting, invoicing, payroll, tax-ready billing and payment reconciliation.
  • Resources and Procurement: Inventory management, procurement and resource tracking.
  • Customer Management: Customer records, communication and WhatsApp customer agents.
