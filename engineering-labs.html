How We Work

A controlled path from idea to handover.

The Engineering Studio workflow establishes requirements, cost, responsibility and acceptance criteria before construction begins.

Client presents an idea

Submit the concept, requirements, reference materials, sketches or existing designs.

Project scoping

We examine requirements, feasibility, hardware, software, materials, labour and development effort.

Design and proposal

We prepare the technical approach, scope, estimated costs, deliverables and implementation timeline.

Approval and deposit

The client reviews and approves the proposal. A deposit is paid before procurement and construction begin.

Material procurement

Either the client purchases materials directly using our specifications, or Gutsy sources them under the agreed arrangement.

Engineering and construction

We develop, assemble, program and integrate the project according to the approved scope.

Testing and validation

The project is tested against agreed functional requirements. In-scope issues are addressed before handover.

Final payment

The client settles the outstanding project balance according to the agreed payment terms.

Packaging and delivery

The project is documented, packaged and dispatched or handed over. Delivery arrangements and costs are agreed in advance.

Procurement arrangement: The selected purchasing arrangement is established before procurement. Material costs, responsibilities and delivery arrangements are included in the project proposal.
Pricing and Commercial Policy

Private quotations. Project-specific pricing.

We do not publish universal prices because engineering projects differ in technical complexity, design requirements, hardware and software, material costs, development time, prototype quantity, testing, documentation, procurement and delivery arrangements.

Quotation components

  • Design and engineering.
  • Software and firmware development.
  • Materials and components.
  • Assembly and construction.
  • Testing and validation.
  • Documentation.
  • Packaging and delivery.

Commercial controls

  • Clients receive a quotation after project scoping.
  • Work proceeds against an approved project scope.
  • A deposit is paid before procurement and construction begin.
  • Material purchasing follows the agreed client-purchase or Gutsy-managed arrangement.
  • Changes outside the approved scope are assessed and quoted separately.
  • Final payment and delivery follow the agreed terms.
